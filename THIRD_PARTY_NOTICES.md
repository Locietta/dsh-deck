# Third-party notices

DSH Deck is MIT-licensed. Its dependencies remain under their own licenses.

## Wry and Tao

The shipping native shell uses Wry and Tao, each available under MIT or Apache-2.0. A binary distribution must include the notices required by these dependencies and their transitive native/Node runtime dependencies. Generate and audit the complete distribution notice set before the first binary release.

- [Wry](https://github.com/tauri-apps/wry)
- [Tao](https://github.com/tauri-apps/tao)
